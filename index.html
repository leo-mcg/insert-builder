      How to print, cut and bind
      1. Print the PDF on US Letter, portrait, at 100% / Actual size (not "fit to page"). Check the 50 mm bar in the top margin with a ruler.
      2. Duplex: flip on the long edge. Use color or grayscale at normal quality so the light guides hold.
      3. Stack the printed sheets in order, sheet 1 on top, fronts up.
      4. Make 5 stack cuts: the 4 outer crop marks, then 1 cut on the middle marks.
      5. Put the bottom pile under the top pile, keeping the order.
      6. Fold so page 1 is the front cover, crease, and staple or stitch through the fold.
